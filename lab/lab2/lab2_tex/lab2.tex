We solved for an analytic solution for order of convergence.
To do this, begin with the expression

\begin{equation}
    \frac{|p_{n+1}-p|}{|p_n-p|^\alpha}=\lambda.
\end{equation}

Nikole taught us a cool trick. Let $\varepsilon_k$ denote $
|p_k-p^*|$, where $p_k$ is the $k^{th}$ iteration of an 
algorithm with $p^*$ being the value we are searching for. 
Then:

\[
\frac{\varepsilon_{k+1}}{\varepsilon_k^\alpha}\approx\lambda
\]

\[
log(\frac{\varepsilon_{k+1}}{\varepsilon_k^\alpha})\approx log(\lambda)
\]
\[
log(\varepsilon_{k+1})-log(\varepsilon_k^\alpha)=log(\lambda)
\]
\[
log(\varepsilon_{k+1})-\alpha log(\varepsilon_k)=log(\lambda)
\]

Now, consider:

\begin{equation}
    log(\varepsilon_{k+1})-\alpha log(\varepsilon_k)=log(\lambda)
    \label{eq:k+1}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
    log(\varepsilon_{k+1})-\alpha log(\varepsilon_k)=log(\lambda)
    \label{eq:k}.
\end{equation}

We subtract \ref{eq:k} from \ref{eq:k+1} to obtain

\[
    log(\varepsilon_{k+1})-log(\varepsilon_k)+\alpha log(
        \varepsilon_{k-1})-\alpha log(\varepsilon_k)=0.
\]

Notice we have eliminated the asymptotic error constant 
$\lambda$. Hence, we obtain

\begin{equation}
    \alpha=\frac{log(\frac{\varepsilon_{k+1}}{\varepsilon_k})}
    {\frac{\varepsilon_k}{\varepsilon_{k-1}}}.
\end{equation}
